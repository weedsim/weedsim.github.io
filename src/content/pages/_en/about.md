I'm Sim HoYeon, and I build game clients with Unity.

## What I'm interested in

- **Game development** — how to design game systems and build them myself.
  Less about translating a design document into code, more about working out
  what kind of skeleton will still hold up when the thing grows.
- **AI** — on-device inference, and what actually works (and what doesn't)
  when you wire AI into a game.
- **Hardware** — how CPUs and GPUs really behave. Why code is fast or slow
  usually comes down to this.
- **Building PCs** — parts, combinations, and measuring the results. A hobby,
  and hands-on practice for the point above.

## About this blog

I write down the problems I hit while building things and how I worked
through them — not just what went well, but also the designs I got wrong and
had to tear out.

I also collect material on things that are new or that I want to try. Even if
it isn't going into a project right now, I'd rather have it written down for
when I need it.
